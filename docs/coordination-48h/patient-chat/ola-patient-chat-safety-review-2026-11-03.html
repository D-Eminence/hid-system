Patient Chat Safety Review

For the owner, through the coordinator · from Ola, clinical owner · 3 November 2026

The patient chat already reads nearly the whole record

This review covers your decision D3 on the patient chat. I checked what the chat does today before designing anything, and what I found changes the question.

"the patient chat should be able to read all medical records especially the one healthcare provider will be using when they open patient profile in ehr" (owner, 3 Nov)

In short

  • The chat production runs is not the careful "release set only" design. That design (hid-system #95) is on hid-system main only and was never deployed. Production runs the staging backend, and its patient chat already reads almost every record from every facility.
  • It reads things a patient should not get without a clinician. These include clinicians' working diagnoses and their free-text notes, the reason a test was ordered, cancelled prescriptions, Lab's correction reasons, and released results such as HIV or pregnancy tests, with nobody there to explain them.
  • Everything goes to the AI service, processed in the US, with nothing removed first. That includes the patient's name and date of birth. The patient is not told about this before asking. No privacy assessment (DPIA) covers the chat.
  • The production settings say the chat is on. Whether those settings are live has not been checked, and DevOps should confirm it today.
  • So D3 mostly confirms what the chat already does. The real work is making it safe. My recommendation is to hide the patient chat page until safeguards S1 to S8 below are in, and to keep the staff chat on.

Where the records go, and what guards them

  • Records go to the Claude API, processed in the US, with no step that removes names, dates of birth or free text. Search questions also send pieces of records to AWS Bedrock.
  • "View source record" can open any record the answer had as evidence, with every field, not only the records the answer cites.
  • One switch turns on both the patient chat and the staff chat, so there is no way to turn off the patient chat alone.
  • An answer that cites a record it was not given is refused. A correct citation does not prove the sentence is right, and short conversational answers are not checked at all.
  • The audit log holds counts only. Nothing records which records were sent to which service.
  • The patient sees no notice before the first question about what is read or who processes it.
  • No DPIA in either repo covers the chat. The only draft leaves online processing out. The privacy notice on the website's main branch, which may not be live yet, mentions "the service behind the Health ID chat assistant" and "other countries", but names neither Anthropic nor the US.
  • Where the chat should be processed is recorded three ways: the code says US, decision 33(1) says worldwide (for the staff chat), and decision 6 plans a move to the EU, not built yet.

Is it switched on?

  • Website: on. The production settings open the Chat page on app. (health config/production.json:19).
  • Backend: the settings say on. The committed production settings turn on AI and US processing for the EHR service, and have since 19 October (hid-system dev branch, infra/terraform/environments/production.tfvars:61 and :223, passed on at modules/application/workloads.tf:281 and :284).
  • Not checked: whether those settings are live. DevOps can confirm in one step: read the live EHR service's settings, or ask one question from a test patient account. We also don't know whether any real patients use the chat today.

Safeguards

S1 to S8 protect the patient and should come before the chat page opens again. S9 to S11 are needed before the chat is relied on at a real facility.

S1Corrections

Results show only once released, and never while a correction waits. In that case the chat says "The lab is correcting this result."

S2Withdrawn records

Nothing entered in error, cancelled, refuted or from a wrong-patient visit. Withdrawing a visit takes everything attached to it. Vitals can be marked entered in error.

S3Finished visits

Signed notes from finished visits only.

S4Sensitive results

HIV, hepatitis, STIs, pregnancy, cancer, genetic and mental health results, and critical results, wait until a clinician presses "Discussed with the patient". I approve the list.

S5Talk to your doctor

The server, not the AI, adds "Talk to your doctor about what this result means" to every answer that uses a result.

S6Other people

No clinicians' free text, no correction reasons, no file names. Health IDs, phone numbers and similar are removed before anything is sent.

S7Send less

The AI gets age and sex, not name and date of birth. The same rule applies to search.

S8Source viewer

It opens only records an answer cited, with safe fields and plain labels.

S9Its own switch

A switch for the patient chat alone, so it can stay off while the staff chat stays on.

S10Tell the patient

A notice before the first question: what is read, who processes it and where, and that answers can be wrong. The backend enforces it.

S11DPIA and sign-off

The DPIA names Anthropic, AWS Bedrock and Textract and one place of processing, and the DPO signs it. Each question's audit row records what was sent where. I sign off each record type after a safety test on made-up records.
