## 6. Session D's route and shape proposal (for B and C to accept or change)

D's full proposal will be `docs/MIGRATE_PAPER_RECORDS_CONTRACT.md` on D's first PR branch. Summary:

- `GET /ocr/patients/:pid/paper-records/availability`
  - returns `{available, reason, coveredUntil, allowance{pagesUsed, pagesLimit, resetsOn, paused}}`;
  - `reason` is one of `under_18 | emergency_access | view_only | no_permission | not_enabled_here | allowance_used_up`.
- **Upload.** Use today's `/ehr/documents/upload-intents` with no `encounterId`, `documentType: "paper_record"` and `paperRecord{pageCount, flaggedPages}`. Reading starts by itself once the virus check is clean.
- `GET /ocr/patients/:pid/paper-records`
  - `status` is one of `checking_for_viruses | reading | ready_to_check | published | couldnt_read | withdrawn`;
  - also returns `checkBy`, `failure`, `canRetry` and published counts.
- `POST /ocr/paper-records/:id/retry`
- `GET /ocr/paper-records/:id/review`
  - **pages:** each with a signed `imageUrl`;
  - **identity:** `nameAsWritten`, `dateOfBirthAsWritten`, `folderNumberAsWritten`, `box`, and `match`, which is one of `matches | differs | no_name | folder_number_only`;
  - **items**, each with:
    - `id`;
    - `kind`, one of `medicine | diagnosis | allergy | note | lab_result | vital`;
    - `asWritten`, `plain` and `dateAsWritten`;
    - `box` (as fractions of the page);
    - `confidence`, one of `clear | check_carefully`, with `checkReason`;
    - `fields`;
    - `prescription{prescriberAsWritten, dateAsWritten}`;
  - plus the saved draft and the short lists for "Change".
- `PUT /ocr/paper-records/:id/review-draft`
- `POST /ocr/paper-records/:id/publish`, with an Idempotency-Key, per-item failures and refusal codes.
- `GET /ehr/patients/:pid/paper-record-items`
- `POST /ehr/paper-record-items/:id/entered-in-error`
- `POST /ocr/paper-records/:id/withdraw`
- Prescriptions get an optional `sourcePaperRecordItemId`.

Who decides what:

- **C** owns the routes, storage and publish rules.
